# Field response and discovery implementation status

Implementation authorized 12 October 2026. This tracks the [accepted plan](field_response_discovery_plan.md) without replacing its goals or scientific acceptance gates. A ✓ means the named bounded component is implemented and verified; it does not imply that its parent milestone or SOTA claim has passed.

## Goals retained throughout implementation

- Instrument-independent, queryable spatial–spectral–temporal fields; catalogs and segmentation remain optional scientific views.
- Native measurements and calibrated response/history; no prerequisite homogenization to the worst PSF.
- Non-Bayesian inference by default, with explicit constraints/regularization and their bias/transfer.
- Correct PSF/ePSF/pixel/flat conventions and physically distinct additive/noise stages.
- Shared nuisance, gauge, selection and uncertainty semantics; unsupported modes or guarantees remain explicit.
- All objects, morphologies, wavelengths and time behavior; no compulsory extragalactic, blob or common-morphology scaffold.
- Poorer/older observations retained as evidence, with no unconditional promise that a misspecified estimator improves.
- Tensor/device/differentiability and survey scale; lean catalog relations; torchfits I/O and torchsky-harness science ownership.
